HOW IT CONNECTS

Speech meets bounded tools.

Browser / Zhengchen→AssemblyAI voice→Voice tools & confirmation→X6 integrations

Camera, Sentinel, ShadowBroker and registry reads connect through the X6 interface. The local calculator follows its own reviewed execution path.

What is available

English voice interaction, camera consent and predictions, Sentinel observations, attributed ShadowBroker headlines, X6 registry reads and reviewed local arithmetic.

Detection predictions can be wrong. Telemetry has source-specific freshness limits. Registry entries do not prove live availability. General distributed execution from Voice remains planned.

Run the Voice application locally

The public repository includes source code, tests, setup instructions and integration contracts. Voice requires AssemblyAI access. Hardware workflows require compatible external services.

The Voice layer and its integrations are the contribution presented here. X6, firmware and external services are pre-existing components.
